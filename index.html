Television Energy Market Overview

Q1. What type of TV screen technologies are currently available in Australia and which are the most frequent?

Most TVs in Australia are LCD (LED) , whereas OLED TVs are less common.

Q2. What screen sizes are available and which are most frequent?

65-inch is the most common size, followed by mid-range sizes around 55 inches.

Q3. Which brands have the largest number of models?

KOGAN, LG, and SAMSUNG dominate the market with the largest number of models.

Q4. Which type of screen technology consumes the least amount of power?

LCD TVs consume the least power, while OLED uses slightly more.

Q5. What is the relationship between screen size and power use?

Larger TVs use more power

Q6. What is the relationship between star rating and screen size?

Star ratings are fairly distributed; bigger TVs can still be efficient.

About This Project

COS30045 Data Visualisation — Unit Demonstration Project

Project Purpose

This website is developed as part of COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology. It acts as an interactive demonstration platform to host web-based data visualisations exploring energy consumption data in Australia.

Technical Stack

  • Frontend: Semantic HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6)
  • Data Pipelines: KNIME Analytics Platform (Dataset cleaning and transformation)
  • Version Control & Hosting: GitHub and Vercel
